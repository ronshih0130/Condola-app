# B-028 — Delivery and notifications

Scope: common event/delivery adapter, existing `email_log` compatibility, delivery-result queries, reminder management and unread/read center in all four apps.

Out of scope: real external sends or assuming queued/API-accepted means delivered.

UI/copy: CAP-P-13/15/22 and brand-v2 message/notification/email templates.

Data/migrations: propose `<unique_timestamp>_b028_notification_delivery_claims.sql` for missing claim uniqueness, read markers and source references.

Acceptance:
- Given an event, when multiple workers claim it, then only one channel attempt is owned.
- Given delivery progress, when displayed, then queued/sent/failed remain distinct and unsupported delivery claims are absent.
- Given a recipient, when marking read, then another recipient’s state is unchanged.
- Given legacy/new reminder paths, when both run, then ownership rules prevent duplicate Email.

Tests/evidence: invite/bill/payment/handover/renewal/report/repair events, retry failures, four-app RLS and local captured templates under `/workspace/e2e/results/B-028/`.
