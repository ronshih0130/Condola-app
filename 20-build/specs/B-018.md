Scope: per-report history, confirmation/rejection/reversal, remaining balance and consistent home status.

Out of scope: claiming a tenant report proves receipt or implementing payment rails.

UI/copy: CAP-T-04/09, brand-v2 tenant home. Preserve “已回報，待管理方確認”.

Data/migrations: none expected; propose `<unique_timestamp>_b018_tenant_payment_history_read.sql` only for additive authorized reads.

Acceptance:

- Given pending/partial/settled/overdue cases, when viewing home and details, then statuses and balance agree.
