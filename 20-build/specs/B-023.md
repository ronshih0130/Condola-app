# B-023 — Owner portal gaps

Scope: authorized document center, lifecycle replies, repair progress, company messaging, profile/security/sessions and notification preferences. Use common portal services rather than duplicate models.

Out of scope: staff permissions, automatic consent from views/downloads or protected text changes.

UI/copy: CAP-O-09/10/11, OWN-04/05/07–09 and brand-v2 owner portal patterns.

Data/migrations: reuse lease/file/thread/preference services; propose `<unique_timestamp>_b023_owner_portal_access.sql` for missing additive reads/reply records.

Acceptance:
- Given owner/property authorization, when listing files/repairs, then only authorized current/history content appears.
- Given a lifecycle reply, when saved, then tenant/company responses remain separate.
- Given view/download, when audited, then no consent/confirmation is inferred.
- Given session/preference edits, when submitted, then ownership and recent-auth controls apply.

Tests/evidence: each route/API/file, multi-property isolation and account cases under `/workspace/e2e/results/B-023/`.
