## Archive

A lease cannot be archived on its own. `public.leases` has no `archived_at` column. `lease_status` is only `draft`, `pending_signature`, `active`, `ended`, `terminated`, or `cancelled`, and none of those values is an archive. The archive command writes `archived_at` on a property, a unit, or a room. Signed-PDF upload and replacement treat a lease as archived only when its property, its unit, or its target room has `archived_at` set.
