# B-012 — Renewal and termination

Scope: separate owner/tenant intent, notice provenance, linked replacement leases, comparison and overlap validation; preserve early-termination history.

Out of scope: silently changing signed terms or treating intent as signature.

UI/copy: CAP-M-13/20, CAP-T-11, J6 and brand-v2 comparison/forms.

Data/migrations: propose `<unique_timestamp>_b012_lease_lifecycle_versions.sql` only for missing links/versioned intents and notices.

Acceptance:
- Given renewal, when comparing, then dates, rent, payment day and deposit changes are explicit.
- Given missing terms or overlapping dates, when submitting, then server rejects.
- Given a completed replacement, when viewing the old lease, then it remains linked and read-only.
- Given notice or intent, when recorded, then source/date/party are retained without implying other-party agreement.

Tests/evidence: date boundaries, concurrency, tenant isolation and new/old condition screenshots under `/workspace/e2e/results/B-012/`.
