## B03 A successful cross-origin redirect can evade the error ledger and reach an external host

collector.cjs 51, 56–62, 68–73 and installed playwright-core/lib/coreBundle.js 37603–37612. The route callback checks only intercepted requests. Chromium Playwright explicitly continues redirected requests without invoking that callback. Both request listeners accept successful foreign hops and journal paths without origin. The default context also allows service workers, and there is no WebSocket handling. Enforce loopback at the independent transport boundary before navigation, record every observed hop and target, disable or reject unsupported transports and worker interception, and treat each foreign attempt as fatal. Include a redirect-origin and socket control.

## B04 Late successful evidence conflicts and observer loss can survive the final cut

## B05 The PW and V8 document attribution can borrow identities from another render
