**Graceful retirement.** The guard records each readable EOF separately. It forwards a FIN only after both decoders have independently seen admissible wire Close frames and the exchange has a bound owner and retirement intent. Upgrade-head bytes are written before checking already-ended read halves. Local FIN forwarding creates no wire Close evidence. Settlement still requires both independently decoded Close frames, the associated lifecycle anchor, parser completion and actual socket closures.

The guard enforces identity equality; the independent parent still has to establish what browser document that identity represents.
