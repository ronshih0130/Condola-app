## Reuse and proposed records

- `public.leases` supplies lease identity, currency, rent amount and frequency, dates, contractual deposit and termination information. `deposit_settled_at` drives retention and must not be set merely because a statement or payer report exists.
- `public.invoices` holds status, period and invoice totals; `public.invoice_lines` holds charge type, amount and service period. `public.payments` holds lease, amount, currency, status, deposit marker and proof file; `public.payment_allocations` records invoice-level allocations. Existing ledger transactions and entries provide separate accounting facts and reversals. These facts must be inspected as applicable: contractual deposit, `is_deposit`, a linked payment or an attachment alone do not establish funds retained, paid or available for settlement.
- `app.handover_records`, versions, attachments and replies from `20261031100214_b011_handover_records.sql` preserve handover history. `public.files` provides an existing organization-scoped file reference. Existing billing, payment and handover records do not by themselves preserve an itemized settlement version, each party's exact-version response, and a distinct payer report with counterparty response.
- Six proposed immutable private tables represent statement versions; typed payment/handover attachment/file source references with factual snapshots; itemized deductions and prior-item references; independent party replies; payer-reported refund/top-up; and responses to that exact report. Composite references bind organization, lease and version where existing keys permit it. Evidence references do not consume or attribute money. A report has no paid/completed default; no response means pending. The proposed response trigger requires the report's named payee and rejects the payer, but is not a substitute for actor authentication and role authorization.

The SQL records whole minor-unit amounts; item values are nonnegative and the signed calculated balance is nullable. The latter column is only a possible future snapshot; **this unvalidated foundation must not supply an authoritative net balance, publication, or outcome completion**. The evidence and basis JSON are snapshots, not proof of correctness. No feature flags, legal text, messaging, ledger posting, money movement, settlement lifecycle marker or external service are changed.

## Ron's policy and unresolved authority

The B-011 policy recorded in `20-build/BLOCKED.md` is manual, evidence-backed admin/accountant attribution with source bounds and no double use; Taiwan monthly known-period full rent or inclusive partial days × monthly rent / 30, capped at monthly rent, rounded half-up to whole TWD once; no duplicate charge for an already billed period. Unknown source or period stays unknown, and there is no implied global or nonmonthly rule. Actual refund/top-up requires payer proof and counterparty acknowledgement; nonresponse remains pending. These are approved policy choices, not an approval of this SQL, execution, source data, staging or release. B-012 old-deposit carryforward and difference-only handling is later, separate work.

**SOURCE AUTHORITY IS NOT SOLVED.** This foundation has no canonical attribution or source consumption record or source-wide uniqueness rule. A future design must establish stable external/payment source identity and uniqueness across leases and existing allocations, manual evidence and actor, same-org/same-lease payment, confirmed status, matching currency, source remaining amount and amount bounds. It must serialize concurrent uses, including a payment row lock and a transactional aggregate of latest-effective allocations/attributions, excluding superseded history but including existing payment allocations. Design replay, corrections, double-use, refunds, revisions, and later carryforward before choosing whether one narrow canonical attribution object is required. Payment foreign key here is only `(org_id, payment_id)`; it does not enforce payment lease, currency or status. File FK is organization-only and does not establish lease or rights to disclose it. Statement currency is not constrained to lease currency. Do not infer source uniqueness or availability from any of these FKs.

Future service design must prove designated tenant/staff/owner and real payer/payee relationships, authenticated actor and session, organization and environment access, expiry/revocation and current feature flags; validate evidence ownership, source changes since snapshot, prior item relation to the actual earlier version, request payload replay, response ordering and independence, current party roles and scope, and any status/consent lifecycle. The proposed rows permit conflicting replies or multiple reports; no rule here chooses a final reply, binds amounts to a calculated balance, prevents duplicate real-world transfers, proves counterparty identity, or marks completion. Decide those semantics before opening access. Snapshot JSON has no validated schema; it must be bounded and validated. Use existing safe upload patterns only after checking attachment privacy and retention; don't expose private sources through a public gateway.

Rent calculation must first establish applicable jurisdiction, monthly frequency, agreed period, occupied dates and the existing nonvoid billed rent lines and relevant adjustments. `invoices.period_start/end` and `invoice_lines.service_period` may be incomplete or ambiguous; fail closed when evidence does not establish a period or existing charge. Tests must cover full and partial periods, February, 31-day periods, mid-period move-out, rounding once, cap, overlapping/duplicate invoices, voids, credits, unknown dates and currency, and must not treat recorded financial facts as cash movements.
