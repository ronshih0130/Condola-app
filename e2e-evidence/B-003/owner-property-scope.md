# Owner and property scope

The payee gateway requires a property-specific bank account to match the lease property, at assignment and on every tenant read. A company account without a property remains eligible under the other authorization rules.

The current implementation permits an administrator to select an active owner contact in the same organization. The available product specification does not establish that the owner must be linked to the lease property. Whether to add that business constraint remains an open policy question for Ron; this implementation does not infer it.
