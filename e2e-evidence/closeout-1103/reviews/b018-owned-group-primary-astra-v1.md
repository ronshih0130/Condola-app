# B018 Darwin server shutdown, primary source assessment

**Conditionally sound for signalling an owned process group. Physical drainage needs separate evidence.** A dedicated parent can keep its direct session leader's numeric PID reserved until the sole consuming wait. This removes the PID/PGID reuse race during that interval. This is a design conclusion for independent review. The existing v2 helper exposes its Popen in a mutable dictionary and has no persistent-server stop operation. It does not establish the proposed conditions.

## Why the address stays owned

Darwin fork(2), setsid(2), intro(2) and setpgid(2) describe unique child PIDs, initial PID=PGID=SID, session-leader restrictions and the same-session restriction on joining an existing group. A new group cannot be created with an arbitrary other process's reserved PID. The wait(2) interface and proc.h's SZOMB state distinguish termination from collection by the parent. Under ordinary child retention, the personal child either still executes or occupies its PID as an uncollected child. The leader need not still be signalable for that numeric reservation to hold. A group signal can reach the remaining original-group members, or report that no eligible group exists. It targets one process group, even though its initial number also identifies the session. It cannot equivalently signal every process in a session or every descendant.

**Sole-reaper contract.** One fresh, single-threaded, pinned controller owns the private strong reference and every consuming wait targeting that child. Launch only a fixed reviewed argv/environment with start_new_session=True, shell=False and an immutable direct-child record. No independently supplied PID, PGID, mutable child mapping, Popen, relaunch-on-error or reconnect from a receipt. Bind opaque identity to this controller instance and launch generation. Return only opaque handle and immutable outcomes. Disallow unreviewed Python startup hooks, environment injection, callbacks, native plugins, SIGCHLD handlers, competing waiters, child watchers, unmanaged background threads and debugger/tracer intervention. A subprocess sibling cannot normally wait on the parent's personal child. In-process code using waitpid(-1) can. Every helper that spawns a census utility must wait only on its own specific child.

Locally installed subprocess.py makes the threat concrete. poll and wait can reap. send_signal can poll first, terminate and kill delegate to it, communicate/context exit can wait, and dropping a live Popen can run its destructor and put it in the module's cleanup list. Keep the strong reference until sole final wait and record its returncode immediately. Do not expose any of those paths or async interruption windows between reap and invalidation. Make the controller state **SIGNALS_CLOSED** before the consuming wait, even if that wait unexpectedly fails. Never signal a number again after that boundary, controller loss, interruption during reap or identity uncertainty.

## One bounded startup check and shutdown protocol

1. **Verify retention before starting a persistent server.** Require the expected SIGCHLD disposition. Darwin's SA_NOCLDWAIT is an independent flag, so reading Python's handler alone is insufficient. A future approved preflight may launch one fixed, pinned, harmless /usr/bin/true through the same owner. Hold the Popen. Before any consuming wait, positively witness the exact direct child as a zombie with its parent PID and initial group. Prefer additionally matching a terminal os.waitid(P_PID, pid, WEXITED|WNOHANG|WNOWAIT) if the exact interpreter provides it. Validate a positive matching PID and terminal code, then reap once and compare the zero exit. A missing child, ECHILD, failed visibility or deadline fails admission. If using waitid probe the exact installed availability and behavior in that separately approved preflight. This establishes effective retention at one point only. The source closure must preclude any subsequent disposition or waiter change. No guessed C struct or ctypes layout is needed.
2. **Bind the real launch.** Publish each private Popen to the registry immediately after success, before fallible close/log/evidence work. Manage partial launches and asynchronous interrupts so they cannot discard an untracked handle. Require positive PID greater than one, parent different from the child group, and initial direct child plus matching getpgid/getsid. Verify using the actual pinned interpreter/OS during separate qualification. On a fast-exit race preserve the handle and quarantine if initial ownership cannot be corroborated. Never recover authority to signal from matching PID/start-time text in a later controller.
3. **Request clean stop first.** After the acquisition observation window, request SIGTERM once on the anchored direct Next CLI PID using os.kill. Its own handler forwards to the actual child, waits and exits. This avoids delivering both a parent-forwarded and an independent group TERM on the normal path. Use a finite monotonic grace deadline. Record intent before the call and outcome afterward. A completed configured server stop can have exit code zero, but it is a requested shutdown. The untouched diagnostic worker's natural exit remains a separate gate. An attempt with indeterminate delivery cannot be relabelled unsignalled.
4. **Bound emergency cleanup under the same anchor.** Before any numeric signal require the unbroken retention interval, private handle, no consuming wait, known original group and authorized target class. On a deadline, a stipulated TERM-to-group then at most one KILL-to-group (or skip directly to the separately prescribed escalation) is safe from unrelated PGID reuse under those conditions. Never send to zero, minus one, a read-back argument, generic PID list, or group after closing the signal interval. Record every attempted delivery including ESRCH and permission failures. Escalation is cleanup and makes the graceful result unavailable. Signal success does not establish member exit. On ownership uncertainty do not signal. A bounded unresolved state quarantines the workspace and forbids destructive teardown, automatic retry or later signalling from saved numeric IDs.
5. **Prove drain before reaping.** Witness the exact retained leader terminal without consumption. Require a successful, complete, bounded, same-user host membership observation positively identifying this exact PPID/PID/PGID and Z state, with no additional members. Repeat as a consistency check. Use the exact Darwin tool's supported field formats and verify its actual visibility (including zombies), complete output/exit status, deadline, numeric bounds and truncation policy. Prefer an independently verified session binding at spawn over assuming a printed sess column's representation. An empty listing, a live leader alone, an inaccessible peer, failed census or merely a closed listener cannot pass. Two listings are still snapshots. A compliant **closed and quiescent child topology** or verified atomic and complete membership observation is required to rule out fork/exit churn producing a false singleton. No other surviving process capable of rejoining the group, reopening protected resources or producing escaped workers may be assumed absent simply because it was not listed. If the census/topology cannot establish this for the exact workload, emit GROUP_DRAIN_UNVERIFIED. A positive group-only observation says nothing about separately detached browser children.
6. **Reap once.** Close signal authority first. Direct waitpid(exact PID, WNOHANG) must return the exact PID with terminal status matching the positive observation. Store immutable exit/signal status and mark Popen reaped. Any mismatch or exception quarantines. A request/receipt should distinguish leader termination, requested graceful stop, fallback signalling, direct reap, original-group drain and independent escaped/detached-child cleanup. Physical workspace cleanup requires all applicable evidence.
