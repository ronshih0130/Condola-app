# B017 private vendor credential retirement v3

**SOURCE_GO_PRIVATE_RETIREMENT_FRAGMENT_ONLY.** The exact SQL, lifecycle matrix, interfaces and status close the reported-cost and legacy-redispatch findings from v1 and v2. This is an inactive source verdict only.

For an active, unrevoked and unreported/unconfirmed dispatch, the leaf rotates its hash and closes the job. Both staff UI modes then permit a deliberate replacement without waiting for expiry. A reported job instead retains its exact operational state: previously eligible staff can still confirm its original cost through the normal job/expense guard. Confirmed, canceled and already inactive jobs remain in their previous state. Rotation makes old tokens unusable by subsequent lookups, including report, upload and photo routes. No replacement credential is minted for distribution and no vendor message is sent. The predicate is derived from locked database fields. The proposed source locks the live parent and actual sandbox scope, then the affected tickets before jobs, rechecks actor authority and updates/deletes only in that scope. Privilege, lock, collision or admission failure must roll back the complete caller. Nothing here approves retirement of a different firm.

**Sharing history is a binding condition.** In the existing trigger, closing an unreported job inserts an invalidation event, clears live approval/description and deletes its live shared-photo junctions. That invalidation does not contain the prior attachment selection. Approval events produced by the current approval path do contain the snapshot; older rows cannot be presumed to have a matching one. Before first use verify that any live approval/description/photo selection to be cleared is covered by the required existing history, including legacy records. Hold enrollment or that transition if coverage cannot be established. Do not silently delete uncovered evidence or carry an old sharing decision onto a new dispatch. The underlying files, ticket attachments, reported costs and completed expense records are separate.

The close branch invokes the existing secret-drop trigger; hash-only updates rely on the explicit scoped deletion. Prove both branches with the actual FORCE-RLS roles and independently verify absence of secrets and denial of the old token. Also prove one real reported-cost confirmation/expense, both legacy/enhanced redispatch workflows and two-client report-versus-retirement behavior. No process may use this fragment before other writers share the parent/ticket/job lock order, old-worker credentials are fenced and already-issued signed URLs expire or are revoked.
