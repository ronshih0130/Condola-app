## Proposed minimal record

One `app.b012_lifecycle_revisions` table holds append-only revisions for a logical `record_id` per org and lease. Its record captures kind, named subject contact and party kind, origin (`party_direct` or `staff_reported`), actual actor and actor kind, source channel and bounded note, asserted event date (nullable when unknown), separately intended date, bounded factual value, withdrawal and change reason, recorded timestamp, request UUID and canonical payload SHA-256. Unknown asserted date stays NULL; recorded time does not fill it. A response points to the exact prior record revision under the same org and lease and is a separate logical record with its own voice. Its subject, kind, origin and response target remain fixed through that record's corrections. Each successor points to the immediately previous revision of that same record, one successor per prior, with unique revision and request identities. Every successor requires a bounded change reason; a root has none and cannot start withdrawn. Withdrawal is another revision using that same reason. A direct-party successor preserves the original direct actor; a staff-reported correction may record a different authorized staff actor and its reason. History is immutable under the proposed ordinary triggers.

The scalar optional `evidence_file_id` uses the actual `(org_id,id)` FK on `public.files`. Optional digest and bounded metadata are descriptive snapshots, not a JSON relationship or proof of authenticity. The FK alone does not establish lease relevance, actor authority, file privacy, possession, delivery or truth. Do not reuse ID documents as lifecycle evidence. A future protected GET must independently validate access and use a conservative default of staff plus the specific named subject; explicitly design any other sharing before exposing it. No public evidence URL or external message is included.

`party_direct` represents the actual authenticated individual speaking for their own named contact, with actor kind matching subject kind; `staff_reported` records a staff actor's report about a named party and source, not that party's direct action. SQL type checks alone do not prove this relationship, source or identity. No staff report can be upgraded into direct assent by revising it: a direct party response is a separate record. One owner, a primary owner or one co-tenant does not represent all owners/tenants; no B-013 repair policy is imported. This table records individual claims, not agreement, legal validity, notice service, signature, termination or change of lease terms. A response FK points to an exact revision but is not a decision to accept it.
