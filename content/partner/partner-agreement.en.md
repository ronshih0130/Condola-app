# 設計夥伴合作備忘錄（草案 v0.1）／Design Partner Agreement (Draft v0.1)

*English translation for reference. If the two versions differ, the Chinese version prevails .*

**Party A (Provider):** Ron Shih, signing personally (see deferred item 1)
**Party B (Design Partner):** ＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿ Tax ID: ＿＿＿＿ Representative: ＿＿＿＿

**1. Purpose and term.** Party A provides the Condola (租多啦) rental-management software to Party B free of charge for a trial period of 6 months, extendable to 12 months by mutual agreement, for one firm managing about 200 rental units. The term starts on the date both parties sign.

**2. Partner commitments.** Party B joins a short feedback call weekly for the first 2 months, then every two weeks with Party A (about 30 minutes) and runs real rent cycles in the product (billing, payment records, reconciliation) using real leases.

**3. Positioning.** Condola is software only. Party A does not manage or sublease property, collect rent or deposits, guarantee rent, or hold any funds. Party B handles all rent and deposit payments itself or through its own financial institution.

**4. Data ownership.** Party B owns its data. Data that Party B enters or generates in the product belongs to Party B. Party B may export it at any time in a common format such as CSV. On Party B's request, or ＿＿ days after this agreement ends (proposed 30 days, to allow export), Party A deletes Party B's data, including backups within the backup cycle, and confirms the deletion in writing.

**5. Personal data protection.** (a) Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, Party B is the data controller (委託機關) and Party A is the processor (受託者). Party A processes personal data only on Party B's instructions and for the purpose of this agreement, under Party B's supervision. (b) Party A uses reasonable security measures, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and access logs. (c) Tenant ID document images are deleted automatically 90 days after the lease ends (expiry or early termination); for leases that never take effect, are cancelled or voided, 90 days after upload; backup copies are purged within 14 days (consistent with section 4 of the Privacy Policy; CEO decision, 2026-10-27). (d) Data is hosted in cloud data centers in Tokyo, Japan (Supabase/Cloudflare). Party B acknowledges and agrees to this cross-border transfer and is responsible for informing tenants as the law requires. Party A gives Party B a list of its sub-processors. These include third-party generative AI services (such as OpenAI, Anthropic and Google), which Party A may engage to help process data. The data may be transferred outside Taiwan and is retained under each provider's data retention policy. Party B acknowledges and agrees. (e) Party A notifies Party B of a personal data breach promptly, and no later than 72 hours after becoming aware of it, and helps Party B meet its duties to notify data subjects and the authorities.

**6. Beta and liability.** The service is a beta provided as-is. Party A does not warrant that it will be uninterrupted or error-free. Liability cap: Deferred (no lawyer engaged for the pilot, per Ron 2026-10-20). Party A's liability for intent or gross negligence is not limited.

**7. Confidentiality.** Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential during this agreement and for ＿＿ years after it ends (proposed 2 years).

**8. Case study.** Party A may name Party B, use its logo, or describe its results as a case study only with Party B's prior written consent.

**9. Pricing after the trial (indicative).** The service is free during the trial. After the trial, the first 4 units are free and billing starts from the 5th unit: Party B pays a founding-partner rate of NT$20 per unit per month (Pro features) for each unit beyond 4, locked for 2 years, so NT$3,920/mo at 200 units ((200 − 4) × NT$20). List price for reference: NT$40 Pro / NT$30 Standard per unit per month. Final terms are set in a separate formal contract. This clause is not binding.

**10. Termination.** Either party may end this agreement at any time with 30 days' written notice. Clauses 4, 5 and 7 survive termination.

**11. Governing law.** This agreement is governed by the laws of Taiwan (R.O.C.). The ＿＿＿＿ District Court (proposed: Taiwan Taipei District Court) is the court of first instance.

**12. Signing.** Until e-signature validity is confirmed, the parties sign on paper and upload the signed PDF for record-keeping.
