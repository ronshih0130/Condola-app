**Party B (Design Partner):** ＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿ Tax ID: ＿＿＿＿ Representative: ＿＿＿＿

**1. Purpose and term.** Party A provides the Condola (租多啦) rental-management software to Party B free of charge during the cooperation period, for one firm managing about 200 rental units. The cooperation period is about 3 months, starting on the date both parties sign and ending February 9, 2027, extendable by the parties' written agreement.

**2. Partner commitments.** Party B joins a short feedback call weekly for the first 2 months, then every two weeks with Party A (about 30 minutes) and runs real rent cycles in the product (billing, payment records, reconciliation) using real leases.

**3. Positioning.** Condola is software only. Party A does not manage or sublease property, does not handle rent, deposits or any other funds, and does not guarantee that tenants pay rent on time. Party B handles all rent and deposit payments itself or through its own financial institution.

**4. Data ownership.** Party B owns its data. Data that Party B enters or generates in the product belongs to Party B. Party B may export it at any time in a common format such as CSV. On Party B's request, or ＿＿ days after this agreement ends (proposed 30 days, to allow export), Party A deletes Party B's data and confirms the deletion in writing; backup copies Party A keeps itself are purged within 14 days, and backups inside the hosting platform are handled under the provider's backup process.

**5. Personal data protection.** (a) Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, Party B is the data controller (委託機關) and Party A is the processor (受託者). Party A processes personal data only on Party B's instructions and for the purpose of this agreement, under Party B's supervision. (b) Party A uses reasonable security measures, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and access logs. (c) Tenant ID document images: for fixed-term leases, deleted automatically 90 days after the lease ends (expiry or early termination); for leases without a fixed term, 90 days after the actual termination or move-out date (the period does not start while the lease is ongoing); for leases that never take effect, are cancelled or voided, 90 days after upload; backup copies Party A keeps itself are purged within 14 days, and backups inside the hosting platform are handled under the provider's backup process (consistent with section 4 of the Privacy Policy; CEO decision, 2026-10-27, revised 2026-10-28). (d) Data is hosted in cloud data centers in Tokyo, Japan (Supabase/Cloudflare). Party B acknowledges and agrees to this cross-border transfer and is responsible for informing tenants as the law requires. Party A gives Party B a list of its sub-processors. These include third-party generative AI services (such as OpenAI, Anthropic and Google), which Party A may engage to help process data. The data may be transferred outside Taiwan and is retained under each provider's data retention policy. Party B acknowledges and agrees. Data handled by these services may include ID document images, national ID numbers and bank account numbers, unmasked. (e) Party A notifies Party B of a personal data breach promptly, and no later than 72 hours after becoming aware of it, and helps Party B meet its duties to notify data subjects and the authorities.

**6. Beta and liability.** The service is a beta provided as-is. Party A does not warrant that it will be uninterrupted or error-free. Liability cap: Deferred (no lawyer engaged for the pilot, per Ron 2026-10-20). Party A's liability for intent or gross negligence is not limited.

**7. Confidentiality.** Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential during this agreement and for ＿＿ years after it ends (proposed 2 years).

**8. Case study.** Party A may name Party B, use its logo, or describe its results as a case study only with Party B's prior written consent.

**9. Pricing.** The Design Partner uses the service free of charge during the cooperation period. After the cooperation period ends, the free plan and pricing are as published on the Condola website; final terms are set out in a separate formal contract to be signed by the parties. This clause is not binding.

**10. Termination.** Either party may end this agreement at any time with 30 days' written notice. This agreement ends when the cooperation period expires, unless the parties agree in writing to extend it. After termination, Party B may export its data under clause 4. Clauses 4, 5 and 7 survive termination.

**11. Governing law.** This agreement is governed by the laws of Taiwan (R.O.C.). The ＿＿＿＿ District Court (proposed: Taiwan Taipei District Court) is the court of first instance.

**12. Signing.** Until e-signature validity is confirmed, the parties sign on paper and upload the signed PDF for record-keeping.
